Ultimately, embracing organization not only improved my academic performance but also significantly enhanced my overall well-being, making the journey far more enjoyable and rewarding.

    Essay

    Topic: LLMs such as ChatGPT, Claude, and CoPilot must be used as a tool not a crutch."

    Large Language Models, are “a category of deep learning models trained on immense amounts of data, making them capable of understanding and generating natural language and other types of content to perform a wide range of tasks.” (Stryker, 2021). Within the business sector, these tools can optimize efficiency and elevate the overall quality of customer service. Similarly, LLMs can enhance educational environments by expanding student access to global information networks, supporting independent research, and providing targeted answers to specific inquiries. LLMs can also be used by a student to get a more detailed understanding of complex topics. Tutors are humans who at times become weary and are not able to provide the same level of personalized attention that each student requires. Class sessions are not sufficient to meet all students' needs, but LLMs can supplement instruction and provide additional support.
    Where these tools are used by students however to replace critical thinking and learning, they become a crutch rather than a tool. This can be detrimental to their learning and development and bad for the working population in the future. Too much dependency on these tools can hinder the development of essential cognitive skills and critical thinking abilities. Overall, LLMs are very useful tools and can significantly enhance the learning experience if used appropriately.
